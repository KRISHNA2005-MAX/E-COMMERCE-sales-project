# 🛒 E-Commerce Sales Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

The **E-Commerce Sales Dashboard** is an interactive business intelligence project developed using Microsoft Power BI to analyze e-commerce sales performance, profitability, product categories, and regional sales distribution.

The primary objective of this project is to transform sales data into meaningful business insights by tracking key performance indicators (KPIs), analyzing monthly profit trends, identifying profitable product sub-categories, and comparing sales across different states.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Microsoft Power BI** – Dashboard development and data visualization
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation
- **DAX** – Calculations and business metrics
- **Data Modeling** – Organizing data for analysis
- **Interactive Visualizations** – Bar charts, pie charts, donut charts, and KPI cards

## 📂 Data Source

The project uses an e-commerce sales dataset containing transaction-level information related to sales amounts, quantities, profits, payment methods, product categories, sub-categories, states, months, and sales representatives.

**Key data fields include:**
- Amount
- Quantity
- Profit
- Payment Mode
- Product Category
- Product Sub-Category
- State
- Month
- Sales Representative

## ✨ Features & Highlights

### 📊 1. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- **Total Sales Amount:** 438K
- **Total Quantity Sold:** 6K
- **Total Profit:** 37K
- **Average Sales Metric:** 121K, as displayed in the dashboard

### 📈 2. Monthly Profit Analysis
- Visualizes profit performance across all 12 months.
- Identifies profitable and loss-making months.
- Helps analyze changes in profitability over time.

### 💳 3. Payment Mode Analysis
- Analyzes the distribution of transactions across payment methods.
- Includes COD, UPI, debit cards, credit cards, and EMI.
- Highlights the contribution of each payment method.

### 👥 4. Sales Representative Performance
- Compares profit generated by different sales representatives.
- Helps identify high-performing and lower-performing representatives.

### 🛍️ 5. Product Sub-Category Analysis
- Compares profit across sub-categories such as printers, bookcases, sarees, accessories, and tables.
- Identifies the sub-categories contributing most to overall profitability.

### 🌍 6. State-Wise Sales Analysis
- Compares sales amounts across Maharashtra, Madhya Pradesh, Uttar Pradesh, Delhi, and other available states.
- Helps identify important regional markets.

### 📦 7. Category-Wise Quantity Analysis
- Visualizes the quantity distribution across Clothing, Electronics, and Furniture.
- Highlights the contribution of each product category.

### 🎛️ 8. Interactive Filters
- Filter the dashboard by state.
- Analyze performance across different quarters.
- Explore sales trends and business metrics interactively.

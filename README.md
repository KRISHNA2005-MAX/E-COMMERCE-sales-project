# E-COMMERCE-sales-project
# Interactive E-Commerce Sales Performance Dashboard



## Project Overview

This project delivers an interactive business intelligence dashboard designed to analyze and visualize e-commerce sales performance across multiple dimensions, including revenue, profitability, regional distribution, and consumer payment behavior. The primary purpose is to help stakeholders track critical KPIs at a glance, uncover seasonal trends, evaluate customer value, and identify high-performing categories to make data-driven retail decisions.

---

## Tech Stack

* **Business Intelligence & Data Visualization:** Microsoft Power BI


* **Data Transformation & Modeling:** Power Query, DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
* **Data Storage / Input:** Microsoft Excel / CSV Dataset

---

## Data Source

* **Dataset:** E-Commerce Online Retail Sales & Orders Dataset (transactional order records covering Indian states and major retail categories).


* **Key Fields Analyzed:** Order Date, Sales Amount, Profit, Order Quantity, Customer Name, Category, Sub-Category, State, Payment Mode, and Quarter.



---

## Key Features & Highlights

* **High-Level Executive KPIs:** Quick summary cards showcasing core business metrics—**438K** Total Sales Amount, **6K** Total Quantity Sold, **37K** Net Profit, and **121K** Average Transaction Value.


* **Monthly Profitability & Trend Analysis:** Clustered bar chart tracking monthly profit variations from January to December, highlighting peaks (e.g., November and January) and dip periods.


* **Top Customer Performance:** Ranked breakdown of the most profitable individual customers (Harivansh, Madhav, Madan Mohan, Shiva, Vishakha).


* **Payment Mode Distribution:** Donut/Pie chart displaying transaction methods, led by Cash on Delivery (COD - 44%), followed by UPI (21%), Debit Card (13%), Credit Card (12%), and EMI (10%).


* **Category & Sub-Category Insights:**
* **Product Category Share:** Visual split showing Clothing dominating sales volume (63%), followed by Electronics (21%) and Furniture (17%).


* **Sub-Category Profitability:** Horizontal bar breakdown highlighting top-earning sub-categories, led by Printers and Bookcases.




* **Geographical Sales Distribution:** Regional comparison mapping sales volume across key states (Maharashtra, Madhya Pradesh, Uttar Pradesh, and Delhi).


* **Interactive Slicers & Filtering:** Slicers for **State** and **Quarter** (Qtr 1 to Qtr 4) allowing dynamic cross-filtering across all visual cards and charts.
